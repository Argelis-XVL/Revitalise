## Transcriber's notes — read these before triaging

1. **Two items in Source 2 are near-duplicates of each other, and one is a duplicate of a third.**
   The unlabelled "In Auto-reject category – can we add these as a category under 'Casework'…"
   item and General item 1 ("Status – auto-pass … Can we add these as a category under
   'Casework'…") carry the same sentence against two different buckets — *auto-reject* and
   *auto-pass*. Read literally, Emily is asking for both automatic outcomes to be routed through
   Casework for a human sense-check. Do not collapse them into one item without confirming which
   buckets she means; the difference decides whether the auto-pass path keeps any straight-through
   behaviour at all.

2. **Source 1 and Source 2 ask for opposite things about Region, and they are not in conflict.**
   Source 1: *"Region shouldn't be visible for Trustees"* and *"include this in the overall grant
   portal accessed by the grant admin and remove from the Trustees"*. Source 2: *"Please can we
   include the region section here"* — "here" being the grant administrator application. One
   requirement, two surfaces: region visible to the grant admin, hidden from trustees.

3. **The £500 / £100 thresholds in Eligibility item 4 are the Client's own statement of a business
   rule, not a fee or rate.** They may be carried into a requirement. `C-COM-004` / D-3 governs
   *this engagement's* fees, rates and bank details, which is a different thing; nothing in this
   file is a commercial term.

4. **Four items are questions, not change requests**, and answering them may close them at zero
   build cost: *"What is included in the anonymised narrative section?"*, *"What does 'No Rounding
   was Applied' mean?"*, *"What does 'Override' section show?"*, *"Quality Monitoring - where are
   these recorded?"*, plus the two access questions (*"Will the grant admin receive all access?"*,
   *"assuming I will have access to all of these?"*). Triage them as answers-first before sizing
   any build.

5. **"as per form" (Eligibility items 2 and 3) is a reference to the live WordPress application
   form, not to a document in this repository.** The authoritative option lists have to be
   ground-truthed against the form itself or against Alex's field export — not guessed from
   `docs/Import/Application Data Export.xlsx`, whose relationship to the current live form is
   unverified as of this transcription.

6. **Eligibility item 7 announces a change to the upstream application form** ("We will be adding
   an additional question"). That is Alex's WordPress work, not Power Platform work, and it is an
   external dependency on the intake contract before the Dataverse side can bind to it.
