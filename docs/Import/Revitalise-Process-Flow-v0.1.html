Nothing is sent, approved, or published without Emily confirming it. These automations replace the data handling, not the decision-making. The gold-bordered steps above are where a human stays firmly in control.

Points to discuss

Open questions to talk through as we walk the flow — the answers shape how each step behaves.

STEP 3 · SCORING

Where should the cut-off sit, and how wide is the "borderline" band Emily reviews by hand?

STEP 4 · ANONYMISATION

What must always be removed, what can be generalised, and how cautious should the AI be before flagging for review?

STEP 6 · ACCEPTANCE

How do we handle applicants who can't sign digitally — is the print-and-post fallback enough?

STEP 5 · BOARD

Will all trustees move to the portal, or do some need the PDF pack for the first few cycles?
