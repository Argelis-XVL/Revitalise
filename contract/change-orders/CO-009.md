## Scope

In the redacted, pseudonymised narrative that trustees read, correct the applicant's own spelling (and
obvious typing slips) so the text is readable. The stored original is never altered; only the
trustee-visible copy is corrected.

**Not part of this order** (in scope of `wbs:5.6`, no change order): fixing grammar that the
anonymisation substitution itself creates, for example he→they giving "they is", or her→them/their.
