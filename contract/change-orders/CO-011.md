retention policies, backup, GDPR documentation and DLP, and no flow. The SDD places the helper under 0.7 and in
the same passage asks the architect to confirm it is inside the estimate. It was not. Under C-COM-002, work no
accepted task covers needs a change order. Finding: IMP-1229.
