## Why an amendment, not a correction

CO-001-A1 priced Amendment A-03 faithfully against the text as it stood when it was drafted, and
said so itself in its own Provenance caveat: sizing was against Amendment A-03 while still
`PROPOSED`, "If the FR text changes materially before that approval, this range should be
re-confirmed against the approved text." Amendment A-03 was approved the same day, and its own
"Resolution (continued)" then withdrew two of the eight items CO-001-A1 priced: NFR-027 in full,
and FR-061's benchmark-comparison clause. CO-001-A1 was not wrong when written — the SDD moved
under it afterwards, the exact pattern `IMP-0288` already generalised into this agent's own
"deferred ROM is provisional by construction" rule. Two independently drafted improvement reviews
caught the same live mismatch the same day; this document is that fix, not a duplicate finding.

Per this project's convention of correcting an approved commercial document with a new one rather
than editing it in place (the same logic C-COM-003 applies to invoices and C-COM-009 applies to
contract sources, and the convention CO-001-A1 itself used against CO-001), CO-001-A1.md is left
as written and this amendment supersedes only its hours line.

## What changed

Both withdrawals are settled, dated reviewer decisions already recorded in
`docs/plans/revitalise-grant-automation-plan.md` — neither is new information this document
investigates:

- **NFR-027 — suppression/grouping helper**: WITHDRAWN by explicit reviewer risk-acceptance
  (Amendment A-03 Resolution, 2026-08-25, plan.md L403, struck through at L783/L789). The
  reviewer's own control (the app's existing field-level security profile) stands in its place;
  nothing is built to replace it.
- **FR-061 — benchmark-comparison clause**: WITHDRAWN the same day (Amendment A-03 Resolution
  (continued), plan.md L434-L450); the applicant-distribution percentages themselves are
  unchanged and remain in scope (plan.md L722). The plan document itself names the consequence:
  "FR-061 is smaller than when that sentence was written... the in-progress sizing pass should be
  sized against the reduced FR-061" (plan.md L1377-L1380).

No hour has been billed against `wbs:6.9` to date — re-confirmed this session by re-running
`scripts/verify-worklog.py` (PASS, 0 violations) and grepping `logs/worklog.jsonl` for `6.9`
(zero matches). This is a second pre-build resizing, not a variance (C-COM-007 does not apply).
