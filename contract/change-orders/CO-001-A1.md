## Why an amendment, not a new change order

CO-001 authorised the content *categories* and deferred the FR/NFR text and firm effort figure to
a follow-up SDD dispatch. That dispatch — plan-agent's Amendment A-03 in
`docs/plans/revitalise-grant-automation-plan.md` — has now authored that text as FR-057–FR-063,
NFR-027 and US-016. Every one of those requirements falls inside the content categories CO-001
already named (application counts, category/percentage breakdowns, funding figures, demographic
and circumstance-score distributions). Nothing new in *kind* arrived — only detail, and it is
larger in scale than CO-001's ROM assumed. Per this project's convention of correcting an approved
commercial document with a new document that supersedes it rather than editing it in place
(`C-COM-003`'s logic for invoices, applied here to change orders), CO-001.md is left as written and
this amendment supersedes only its hours line.

## What changed

CO-001's original 5–8h ROM was sized by analogy to `wbs:6.2`/`wbs:6.3` (3–5h each) before any
FR/NFR text existed, from a summary reading of the two source decks. Amendment A-03 (PROPOSED at
plan-agent's own gate as of this writing) has since derived the actual catalogue in full: 7
functional requirements, one cross-cutting NFR, and one user story wrapping them. Two of the seven
(FR-061, FR-062) are chart-visualisation work with no comparable task anywhere in `wbs:6.1`–`6.8`
to analogise from — the SDD's own words: "two of which... are chart-visualisation work rather than
a straight extension of WBS 6.2's existing list query."

The reviewer confirmed directly that this is additional scope: *"Yes, this is extra scope not
delivered initially."*

## What is deliberately not priced in

Three open questions from Amendment A-03 could push this higher and are not folded into the range
above:

- **OQ-036** — if FR-063's capacity/legacy-split figures need a new manually-maintained input
  rather than reading existing records, that is further follow-on scope, not covered by the 1–2h
  quoted for FR-063.
- **OQ-037** — sourcing and maintaining the UK demographic benchmark dataset behind FR-061 is
  unscoped.
- **OQ-035** — NFR-027's suppression threshold is unset; the 1–1.5h assumes a simple configured
  minimum, not a DPO-driven variable rule.

## Provenance caveat

This sizing is against Amendment A-03 while it was still `PROPOSED` at plan-agent's own gate (not
yet approved into the committed SDD). If the FR text changes materially before that approval, this
range should be re-confirmed against the approved text.
