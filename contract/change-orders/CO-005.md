## Scope

The reviewer confirmed 2026-09-18 (`docs/plans/emily-review-feedback-2026-09-plan.md` lines
1267–1275) that the *Applications per day* tile is **kept**, not dropped, but its calculation
changes:

- Recompute from **all historic applications**, not only the current round.
- Break the result down **month by month**.
- **Anomaly highlighting** — flag any month whose count is a sudden spike or drop against its
  trailing six months.
- Two new rows on `rev_setting`: (a) the date grant applications began being tracked/granted, (b)
  the count of applications that predate this system's use — so historic totals are not silently
  understated, since this system started later than the charity itself did.

## Why this is a fresh change order, not an amendment to CO-001

`wbs:6.9` (`contract/change-orders/CO-001.md`) priced a round-statistics landing screen including an
*Applications per day* tile scoped to the **current round**. This rescope is not a tile removal — it
is a new calculation over a different, larger dataset (all history, not one round), a new
presentation shape (month-by-month, not a single figure), a new analytical step (anomaly detection
against a trailing window), and two new settings the charity's data model does not currently carry.
Per the reviewer's own instruction (plan line 1274): "This is materially larger than the
`wbs:6.9`/CO-001 tile removal §5a priced... so it is added to §5b as a fourth item, not closed as
answer-only" — routed here as a fresh `C-COM-002` decision, not a numbered amendment, matching
CO-001-A1/A2's convention of amending only when the underlying FR is unchanged in kind.
